## 🏗️ Product Suite

AceOS is a four-module student operating system:

| Module | Role | Core Job |
|---|---|---|
| **ScoreBoost AP** | Exam mastery | Diagnose AP readiness, generate study plans, improve AP scores |
| **GradeGuard** | Daily academics | Track grades, calculate GPA, flag risk, and connect classroom performance to study actions |
| **StudySensei** | AI tutoring | Deliver guided tutoring, active recall, and concept remediation |
| **SmartPack** | Social accountability | Turn studying into a team sport with squads, competition, and accountability |

### How the Modules Work Together

- **GradeGuard → ScoreBoost AP:** Weak classroom performance reprioritizes AP study plans.
- **StudySensei → GradeGuard:** Tutor-detected gaps feed follow-up review recommendations.
- **SmartPack → All:** Squad behavior and shared weak spots create accountability and social reinforcement.
- **All → Student Intelligence Profile:** Every interaction sharpens recommendations, pacing, and predicted outcomes.

## ⚠️ Product Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Content/legal exposure** | Medium | High | Use original, aligned content and legal review |
| **AI grading quality misses expectations** | Medium | High | Human audit loops and score-quality thresholds |
| **High AI cost at scale** | High | Medium | Tiered model economics and upsell discipline |
| **Low language adoption** | Medium | Low | Ship Spanish first and gate other languages on adoption |
| **Scope creep** | High | High | Phase gates, milestone discipline, and ruthless sequencing |
| **Student trust/privacy issues** | Low | High | Parent visibility by consent, not surveillance |
